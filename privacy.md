# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 6, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how bt sender defender 5000 handles information when you use the Bot on Discord.

## 1. Information We Process

When you use the Bot, it may temporarily process information provided through Discord, including:

* Your Discord username or display name.
* Messages that are relevant to the Bot's functionality.

The Bot does **not permanently store** this information in a database or other persistent storage.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the Bot is used only to provide its functionality and respond to commands or messages.

We do not use this information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

## 3. Data Storage

The Bot does not maintain a permanent database containing users' messages or usernames.

If information is temporarily processed while the Bot is operating, it is not intentionally retained after it is no longer required for the Bot's functionality.

## 4. Third Parties

The Bot operates through Discord. Discord may independently collect and process information according to its own privacy policies.

We do not sell or share users' information with third parties for advertising purposes.

## 5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to prevent unauthorized access to information processed by the Bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended to circumvent Discord's age requirements or policies. Users must comply with Discord's applicable age requirements and rules.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's functionality or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

## 8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the Bot's handling of information, please contact the Bot's owner through the Bot's Discord profile or support server.
